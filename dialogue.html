Dialogue & feedback

Join the inquiry

I do not claim to have all the answers. I am continuing to explore questions around human potential, Right Education, creativity, sustainability, consciousness, cooperation and complementarity. I would value your time, experience and perspective.

Poll 1 — Human Potential

To what extent do you believe that the conventional education and work systems currently available enable people to develop their natural potential?

Poll 2 — Education from the Inside Out

Which approach do you think should receive greater emphasis in education?

Poll 3 — Competition and Relationships

From your own experience, how does a strongly competitive environment generally affect cooperation and relationships?

Poll 4 — Unhealthy Competition

Have you experienced situations where unhealthy or excessively competitive behaviour reduced trust, cooperation or collective performance?

Poll 5 — Complementarity of Abilities

When people have different abilities, perspectives or roles, what tends to produce better collective outcomes?

Poll 6 — Outer and Inner Development

How important do you think inner development—such as awareness, values, attitudes and self-understanding—is to sustainable improvement in individuals and society?

Poll 7 — Meaningful Development

Which description comes closest to your understanding of meaningful development?

Open-ended Question 1

What have your own life experiences taught you about human potential, education, competition, cooperation and complementary relationships?

Open-ended Question 2

What important question about human development, education, sustainability or consciousness would you like me to explore or research?
