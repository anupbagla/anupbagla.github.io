Right Education

Education from the inside out

I am exploring an approach to education that begins by recognising the unique natural potential of the learner and cultivates thinking, creativity, character, relationship and meaningful action.

Discover

Recognise natural abilities, tendencies, interests and latent potential.

Cultivate

Develop independent thinking, creativity, values, confidence, discipline and continuous learning.

Express

Enable capabilities to find constructive expression in work, relationships, society and contribution.

Questions I want to pursue

How can education help people discover their unique natural potential?

How can creativity and independent thinking become central to learning and development?

How can young people find work or occupations more closely aligned with natural talents and tendencies?

How can right understanding improve recognition, relationships, cooperation and social coexistence?
