Human & Social Development

Exploring relationships, recognition, cooperation, mutual coexistence—with each complementing the other—and ways of moving away from unhealthy, excessively competitive patterns towards more cooperative and complementary forms of human interaction and development.

Complementarity in nature and human systems: Just as different organs and systems of the human body perform distinct functions while cooperating and complementing one another for the health of the whole, human beings and social systems can recognise their differences as potentially complementary rather than merely competitive. This principle of cooperation and complementarity is reflected in natural and biological systems and offers an important direction for healthier human relationships and development.
