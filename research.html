Research & writing

A continuing body of inquiry

My research and writing bring together fields that are often treated separately: process engineering, energy and environment, sustainability, creativity, education, human and social development, and the relationship between outer systems and inner development.

Energy & Environment

Energy management, conservation, environmental auditing, process optimisation, environmental impact assessment and sustainable development.

Creativity & Education

Creative thinking, attitudinal development, Right Education and the cultivation of human capabilities for meaningful and sustainable development.

Human & Social Development

Exploring recognition, relationships, cooperation, complementarity, meaningful work, human potential and healthier forms of social development.

A connecting question: how can engineering, industry, education and social systems support the fuller development of human beings while remaining responsible to society and nature?
