My unique contribution

Connecting what is usually treated separately

I believe my particular contribution lies in bringing together engineering and human development, process and creativity, sustainability and education, outer systems and inner consciousness.

The central inquiry

“How can human beings, human systems—including outer engineering constructs—and society develop from the inside out, in harmony with one another and with nature?”

The connections I want to explore

Engineering ↔ Human Development

Outer engineering can lead toward inner engineering development: systems and technologies ultimately exist to serve human life and development.

Process Thinking ↔ Creativity

Systematic thinking together with lateral, original and creative thinking can open better possibilities.

Economic Development ↔ Sustainability

Development needs environmental responsibility, social value and long-term sustainability, not only production or consumption.

Education ↔ Human Potential

Education can move beyond filling minds with information to discovering and developing natural abilities, talents, thinking and potential.

Outer Development ↔ Inner Development

Lasting improvement requires external systems as well as consciousness, attitudes, values and human relationships.

Harmony ↔ Skilled Action

Harmony and balance within leading to progressively better performance and skillful action—“Yogah Karmasu Kaushalam”.

Perhaps my deepest contribution

“To help human beings and human systems evolve from the inside out—by awakening their inherent potential, capabilities, creativity, understanding and consciousness, and by applying these capacities to build a more humane, equitable, sustainable and meaningful world without fear and exploitation.”

I do not claim to possess all the answers. I see myself as a researcher, learner, seeker, thinker and practitioner. My contribution may not be one single invention, system, professional activity or project initiative. It may be a continuing body of work that brings together engineering and systems thinking; process improvement and innovation; creativity and lateral thinking; energy and environmental responsibility; sustainable development; human and social development; Right Education and human potential; writing and research; mentoring and dialogue; and inner development and consciousness.

My aspiration is not simply to achieve more personally in any manner, but perhaps to participate in work that helps human beings discover their inherent capacities, think independently, become financially independent by doing what one loves to do, develop more fully and contribute towards a better world. And through this, I find meaning and purpose in life and a feeling of being rewarded, fulfilled and contented in life.

Cooperation, complementarity and mutual coexistence

I am increasingly becoming more and more interested through decades of my experiences, in how human relationships and social systems can move away from unhealthy, excessively competitive patterns towards cooperation, complementarity and mutual coexistence—where different individuals and their unique abilities can complement one another rather than continuously compete.

Nature and biological systems offer a powerful illustration of this principle. Different organs and systems of the human body perform distinct functions while cooperating and complementing one another for the health of the whole. I believe that human beings and social systems can learn from such interdependence: differences need not produce rivalry; they can become sources of complementary strength, cooperation and mutual development, which can lead to overall social health, social justice and living harmoniously without fear.

I do not see this as a finished answer. It is an evolving inquiry that I hope to deepen through living, reflection, research, writing, dialogue and collaboration.
