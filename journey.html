My journey

From engineering and systems to human development and consciousness

My professional and personal journey has moved through engineering, process problem-solving, R&D, energy and environmental conservation, sustainability, creativity, human and social development, education and a continuing search for meaning and spiritual realization.

1977–1989

Process Engineering · R&D · Technical Services

Work across instrumentation, chemical and rubber industries and research, developing capabilities in process control, quality, plant engineering, troubleshooting, product testing and technical problem-solving.

1989–1997

Energy · Environment · Project & Training Work

Research and project work with ASCI, Baroda Productivity Council and industry, including energy management, environmental issues, process and plant engineering, reports, proposals, counselling and training.

1997–2014

Independent Consulting · Sustainability

Energy and environmental audits, EIA/EMP, process assessments, conservation studies, infrastructure environmental work and sustainability-related consulting.

2014–today

Freelance Writer · Scholar · Researcher · Seeker

Increasing focus on creativity, human, social and sustainable development, research and writing, alongside a deeper inquiry into consciousness, meaning, spiritual realization and purposeful contribution.

A lesson from the journey: experiences of being misunderstood, lack of recognition, opposition and financial difficulty have also become sources of inquiry into human relationships, communication, recognition, natural abilities, cooperation and healthier development.
A personal milestone · 31 August 2026

At 72: past journey, present junction and road ahead

My 72nd birthday became an occasion not merely to mark another year, but to reflect on what has shaped me and on the conscious actions through which the road ahead can now be built.

The continuing sankalpa: deepen Being; write, research and create; advance Right Education and Sustainable Development; serve and mentor; and allow present conscious action to shape the years ahead.
