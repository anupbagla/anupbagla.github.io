Projects & initiatives

From ideas and inquiry towards practical application

Some of my work begins with a question, develops through research and dialogue, and may eventually move towards practical application. The initiatives presented here are at different stages—from continuing educational and human-development work to concepts under active development.

They are presented not as finished achievements, but as a continuing journey from concept → inquiry → development → possible implementation and contribution.

1. Right Education Project

Active · developing

From discovering human potential to meaningful learning and contribution

Background & need. Education often begins with prescribed curricula, examinations, competition and career pathways before adequately helping learners understand their own abilities, interests, creativity and potential. This raises a more fundamental question: Can education begin with understanding and developing the individual, rather than primarily fitting the individual into predetermined structures?

Concept & approach. The Right Education Project explores an inside-out approach to education—helping learners discover and develop their inherent abilities, independent thinking, creativity, character and capacity for meaningful work and responsible participation in society. The project builds upon my earlier writing and inquiry into Right Education, creativity, attitudinal development and human potential.

Present status. Earlier ideas and writings are being revisited and connected with current thinking on human development, meaningful work and sustainable living.

Next direction. Develop the underlying framework more systematically through research, writing, dialogue, educational material and potentially small-scale mentoring or learning initiatives.

Possible collaboration. Educators, schools, universities, researchers, mentors, youth organisations and others interested in education centred on the fuller development of human potential.

2. Human-Centric Capability Project

Research · framework development

Exploring enterprise as a means of developing people—not merely using people as resources

Background & need. Industry and organisations routinely measure production, productivity, profitability and economic value. A less frequently examined question is whether economic activity can also contribute deliberately to the development of the people participating in it.

This leads to a central inquiry: Can enterprise create economic value while simultaneously strengthening human capabilities, meaningful contribution and development?

Concept & approach. The Human-Centric Capability Project develops this inquiry through an emerging conceptual framework connecting industry, work, human capability and development.

Current working concepts include Human Capability–Centered Enterprise (HCCE), Money-to-Man Development Model (MMDM), Enterprise as a Human Development System (EHDS) and People-First Value Creation Framework (PVCF). These are being examined in relation to established work on stakeholder theory, human capital, learning organisations, sustainability and organisational development.

Present status. Research and conceptual framework under development. The related Industry & Human Development research article is being developed alongside this broader project.

Next direction. Strengthen the literature review and research gap, consolidate the conceptual framework, develop measurable constructs or hypotheses, and explore eventual validation through industry evidence or case studies.

Possible collaboration. Universities, management and industrial researchers, enterprises, sustainability professionals, organisational-development practitioners and organisations interested in more human-centred forms of enterprise.
